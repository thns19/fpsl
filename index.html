How It Works

Your squad has four starters on the Pitchball court and one substitute on the bench. Choose carefully before the next matchday begins.

01
Build a squad
Choose exactly five real PSL6 players within the 40.0m budget.
02
Set your lineup
Place four players on the court and one substitute on the bench.
03
Lock for matchday
When a matchday starts, transfers are locked until the administrator ends it.
04
Track the season
Matchday points accumulate across the season on the leaderboard and player stats pages.
League Rules
  • Squads contain exactly five players.
  • The maximum squad budget is 40.0m.
  • Four players are starters and one is a substitute.
  • Transfers close when a matchday becomes active.
  • Administrators enter player matchday results.
  • Player and manager totals include all completed matchdays.
Draft Scoring System

This is a first draft of the scoring rules — point values are easy to tune once we see how real match stats look. Nothing here is final.
