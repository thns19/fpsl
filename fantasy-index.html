Fantasy

Draft real PSL6 players onto your squad, score points off their real match performances, and compete for the top of the Fantasy leaderboard.

How It Works
01
Build a squad
Pick 5–7 real PSL6 players across different teams within a fixed budget, drawn from their live ratings.
02
Set your lineup
Before each gameweek, choose your starters and bench — and pick one Captain, who scores double.
03
Score off real matches
Every PSL6 fixture that gameweek feeds your players' points based on how they actually performed.
04
Climb the table
Points accumulate across the season on the Fantasy leaderboard, alongside head-to-head mini-leagues.
Draft Scoring System
ActionPoints
Point scored (clean)+2
Point scored (opponent forced into error)+1
Own goal−1
Match win (starter/sub)+4/+2
Clean sheet+3
Man of the Match+2
Captain×2 all points

This is a first draft of the scoring rules — point values are easy to tune once we see how real match stats look. Nothing here is final.
